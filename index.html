The Build

Good parts, wired right, set up by hand.

A partscaster is only as good as the person putting it together. Here’s what every D’Cruz guitar goes through before it’s called finished.

  1. 01

    Component selection

    Warmoth bodies, one-piece maple necks, and quality hardware — chosen so the platform is right before a single wire is soldered.

  2. 02

    Handwound pickups

    Carparelli handwound humbuckers and singles, voiced for output and clarity — the difference between a parts guitar and a guitar with a voice.

  3. 03

    Custom wiring

    Killswitch, master volume and tone, tidy shielded electronics — soldered by hand and wired the way you actually play.

  4. 04

    Professional setup

    Action, intonation, and fretwork dialed in so it plays easily out of the case. Every guitar leaves set up and ready to go.

About

Built and set up in Belleville, Ontario.

D’Cruz Guitars builds custom electric guitars and partscasters — assembled from quality components, wired by hand, and professionally set up so they play easily the moment you plug in. The idea is simple: a properly built, properly set-up electric shouldn’t cost boutique money.

Alongside complete guitars, the shop offers the parts to build or upgrade your own — handwound pickups, bridges, switches, tuners, and the D’Cruz Precision Tuner pedal. From an entry-level Squier partscaster to a fully bespoke build, every guitar is set up by hand before it ships.
